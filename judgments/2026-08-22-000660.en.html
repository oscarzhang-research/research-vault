Thesis in one sentence

SK hynix remains severely undervalued — beyond the sector-wide reasons — because SK hynix is trying to build a memory architecture that raises customization and makes itself irreplaceable, which will transform the traditionally strong cyclicality of memory. At the same time, its shareholder return plan has now formally begun execution.

Core logic

  • SK hynix announced a ₩40 trillion buyback plan (directly retiring shares), which has directly boosted market confidence and genuinely lifted EPS. (The market now trusts more in SK hynix's execution capability for returning excess cash going forward.)
  • The various memory architecture standards and concepts SK hynix has announced are trying to shift memory from a plain commodity toward customization, positioning the company as a standard-setter or the leader of a new architecture (including memory tiering + OCP standards + CPO-direct-attached memory pools + customized services with major customers on the HBM base die). Its irreplaceability is likely to strengthen further.
  • Currently there is almost no disagreement among investment banks on SK hynix's 2026E revenue and operating profit — this shows that its long-term agreements have made earnings extremely visible (mainstream consensus is ₩365 trillion in revenue and ₩265 trillion in operating profit, slightly above my estimate in my previous note). Where they actually disagree is on how much free cash flow SK hynix will generate going forward, since the company has committed to returning at least 50% of FCF to shareholders — so future FCF directly drives EPS. I have to admit I haven't yet done a detailed study of the changes in net working capital; I'll add that once I have (capex and depreciation are relatively clear).

Key assumptions and tracking signals

  • ☐Whether SK hynix can keep announcing continued shareholder return plans going forward
  • ☐Whether SK hynix can keep making new progress on memory architecture or standards
  • ☐Whether SK hynix can keep deepening memory customization going forward

What would prove me wrong

If the following happens, I'll admit I was wrong:

  1. SK hynix fails to keep announcing continued shareholder return plans going forward
  2. SK hynix fails to keep making new progress on memory architecture or standards
  3. SK hynix fails to keep deepening memory customization going forward (memory remains a substitutable, strongly cyclical commodity)

Where I differ from consensus

What the market thinks: memory is a substitutable, strongly cyclical commodity.

Why I disagree: the degree of memory customization will keep rising (fundamentally, memory is becoming ever more important).
